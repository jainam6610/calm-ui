Privacy Policy — Student Launcher

App: Student Launcher (package com.studentlauncher) · Developer: thats.jainam (individual developer) · Effective date: October 8, 2026

Short version: no data leaves your device. Student Launcher has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. The app is not given internet access (it has no INTERNET permission), so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

If you tap a link inside the app (for example this policy), that link opens in your browser, which then loads the page. The app itself still sends nothing.

What the app stores on your device

Everything below is written to the app's private storage on your phone only:

  • Settings and layout — theme, accent, motion, wallpaper choice and edit values, pinned apps, home order, widget placement.
  • Plan (tasks and events) — task title, course, due date, priority and completion state; your event list.
  • Widget content you type — this can include personal things such as expense entries and budget, grades / GPA rows, habits, water logs, flashcards, assignments, class schedule, formulas and reminders.
  • Focus / mindful numbers — daily budgets, per-app limits, pause counts and the minutes shown in the Today and Mindful widgets.
  • Your PIN (if you set one) — only a salted hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) is stored. The PIN itself is never written down anywhere, and it is never sent anywhere.
  • Wallpaper photos you pick — a copy is placed in the app's private storage when you choose it. Original photos are not kept and their locations are not remembered.
  • The list of apps installed on your phone — used only to draw your home screen and app list.

None of this is transmitted off the device. None of it is shared with third parties, because the app has no third-party code in it.

How the usage numbers are produced

The minutes shown for "distracting" apps are counted by this launcher itself: it starts a timer when you open an app from the launcher's home screen and stops it when you come back. The app does not use Android's usage-access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS), no accessibility service and no overlay — none of those permissions are requested. The result is stored only in the app's private storage and is never uploaded.

Permissions we request

  • Notifications (optional, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to show the focus reminder ("10 min on …") while you are in an app you marked as distracting. It is requested only when you turn reminders on, and you can withdraw it at any time in system settings. If you deny it, everything else in the app still works; only the reminder is missing.
  • Notification access (optional, special access): if you grant it, the app's notification listener reads metadata only — the app package, category, post time, group-summary flag and importance. It never reads the title, body, people, icons or any other notification content. The access is off until you grant it in system settings, and you can revoke it at any time; the launcher works without it.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS is the only runtime permission the app requests. Notification access is a separate, optional special access you grant in Android Settings; it is not a manifest permission and the app cannot use it until you turn it on.

There is no location, contacts, calendar, SMS, microphone, camera, storage, phone or usage-access permission.

Installed apps

The app reads the list of installed apps only to show your home screen and app list. This list stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. The app does not use QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.

Photos

Wallpaper photos are chosen with Android's system photo picker. The app never reads your whole gallery — it only receives the photos you select, and keeps a copy of each in its own private storage for the wallpaper.

Third-party widgets

Android widgets you add are provided by their own apps and follow their own privacy policies. This app does not see or forward their content.

Children and young users

Student Launcher does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children: no name, no age, no email address, no contacts and no location. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user-generated content sharing and no third-party analytics. Younger users should ask a parent or guardian before installing any app.

Security

All data sits in the app's private storage, which other apps on the device cannot read. If you set a PIN, only its salted hash is kept, and repeated wrong tries lock the keypad for a growing cool-down. Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for this app are disabled (allowBackup is off and backup/transfer rules exclude all app data), so nothing is copied to Google Drive or a new phone.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the device only for as long as the app is installed. You delete it yourself, at any time, in either of these ways:

  • Settings → Apps → Student Launcher → Storage → Clear storage (removes every setting, task, widget entry, log and wallpaper copy), or
  • Uninstall the app (app-private storage is removed with it).

There is currently no separate "delete my data" button inside the app — clearing storage or uninstalling is the complete deletion path. Because we never receive your data, we have no copy to delete and cannot receive deletion requests; if that ever changes, this policy will say so before it happens.

Wellbeing features

Mindful Mode, the breathe step, brain tasks, focus timers and usage budgets are self-help and productivity tools only. They are not medical, diagnostic, therapeutic or mental-health treatment, and nothing in the app is medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional about health concerns.

Changes to this policy
